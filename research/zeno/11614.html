Bukharhudat AR drachm. With name Turghar.
A very rare, or if telling more correctly one of the most rare coins of Bukharhudat type, with a name of Sogdian governor Turghar. (Turghar is a governor which has rendered significant resistance to the Arabian invasion in Central Asia)
This type of coins is published in several works of O.Smirnova and A.Belenitskiy. In their works these two authors speak on the various points of view about these coins, their origin, aura of circulation, types and classification. But, in any event, we shall try to generalize the basic sense of these works, and to give you some information, which is for certainty accessible only for few considering that these publications are rare.
As known from old China and others historical written sources, the government of Turghar is exactly dated, and related to the period 738 - 750 AD. From the same sources it is visible, that the government of Turghar can be divided into two stages.
The first stage, is when Turghar's father Ikhshid (Ikhshid is a title of Sogdian governors) Gurek in 731 AD asks to ratify his son as a governor of Kabudan and where he becomes a governor on behalf of the father up to the Gurek death.
To the second stage of Turghar government, it is possible to relate the period after the death of Gurek in 738 AD when Turghar gets to the throne, gets the title Ikhshid and rules the Samarkand Sogd down to 750 years.
Paying attention to these brief items of information from a history, and according to the bronze coins of the previous governors, it is visible, that all coins of Samarkand governors have the title Ikhshid. And version that the Sogdian governor Turghar issued drachms of Bukharhudat type with his own name when he was a governor of Kabudan during the period of 731-738 AD is more probable, as on the given coin there is only the name, but the title Ikhshid that was accepted by him in 738 is absent.
O.Smirnova and A.Belenitskiy in their works agreed in one opinion, that during the period of Turghar's government the currency reform of drachms of Bukharhudat type was made. The larger anonymous Bukharhudat drahms of so-called Mugh's type were changed into drahms more small in size, but with the names of the governors.
And the given coin is a confirmation of this theory.
During Ikhshid Turghar (738-750 AD.) there were also issued coins on the Chinese sample (bronze with a square aperture). The first, earlier type, on metal and registration is much similar to coins of previous Sogdian governors. And more later type undergoes the certain changes. The coins become fine, and also appreciablly worsen structure of metal. Probably, the coins of late type were in use for a certain time after death of Turghar.
Rarity:
From the various publications, and information, which is accessible to me from quite
competent sources, these coins are known a little. Namely:
- 1 coin stores in a State museum Hermitage (Saint-Petersberg, Russia)
- 3 coins - are known from excavation on Pendjikent town in the period of 1955-1962. in a museum of a history in Dushanbe, Tadjikistan.
- 2 coins - in a museum of a history of Uzbekistan (Tashkent). From excavation on Paykend 198x,
- 1 coin in a private collection. Uzbekistan. (considerably worse Status, also is not accessible to sale)
- 1 coin - item #3956295889 at eBay.com. This coin has appeared in the market in 2001, and was published later.
Status of a coin.
Proceeding from the images in the various publications of this type of coins, our coin is one of best on safety.

Obverse: Crowned bust of ruler right, circle above crown. In front of the face Sogdian legend - "Turghar", behind the head distorted pehlevi legend.
Reverse: In the center of the coin altar with a head of Ahura Mazda. On both sides of the altar figures of attendants (mobeds).

This coin was described in The catalogue of coins of Penjikend #15 by O.Smirnova.
A.Belinitskiy. Klad serebryanyh monet iz Penjikenda. / Epigraphika of East #17, 1966.
