The significance of the "Kasmir-Samast" designation in general is very questionable. It may just represent a find-spot were small copper coins were preserved. Besides the small enigmatic "Kasmir-Samast" copper coins found there, more ordinary copper and silver from various dynasties are found there. Even if this particular coin was found in Kasmir-Samast, it should be listed in South Sogdh.
