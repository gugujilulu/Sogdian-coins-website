Comments by Alexander Naymark:
This is absolutely correct. These coins were minted in Ishtihan, where Samarqand Ikhshids starting with Ughrak maintained their court. Also, since my last note on these coins, it became clear that the name on them should be read as 'yrt - Airit or Airat [Yazid was De Goyes conjecture in his edition of Tabari].
