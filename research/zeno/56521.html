Dear kabarna, a very good observation!
The tamgha on this, and Smir type 43 coins are not that of Samarqand, probably not even an early or primitive variation (since the Samarqand tamgha is known on coins from at least AD 400). Perhaps the ruler/principality using this tamgha should be located further to the east (Ustrushana?). In any case, the coins ought to be listed together (in Ustrushana category?), perhaps together with such type as #86940 (althought it has the lower part of the tamgha bending to the left).
