I've recently purchased 2 fake AR drachms (Early Bukharkhoda) with identical dies from the same source, as well as a fake Khwarismian drachm of Savshafan. The coins were purchased from a well known dealer of Central Asian forgeries, Igor Khabak (kh.andson), who sells regularly on ebay and directly from Michigan.

He in turn purchased dozens, perhaps hundreds, of central Asian forgeries from Oleg Lyebchenko- when coins are returned to him as forgeries, he is known to resell them to unsuspecting buyers. Igor Khabak also sells forged cast bronzes from Sogdiana, and a number of Bukharkhoda AR drachms cast from the same mould. More than half a dozen complaints have been filed against him. He continuous to sell such coins on ebay. Reputable Sogdian numismatists assert that even the low grade Sogdiana (Chach, Sogdiana, etc.) bronzes which he sells are forgeries.

Very interesting, thank you.
I had seen the messages about this seller on the Yahoo Islamic Coin Group but not here on Zeno.
Because many of the genuine coin finds in Central Asia are harshly cleaned, leaving no visible patina, it is now very difficult to know which coins are genuine and which are fake. The market is damaged by the fake products.
One reminder warning - it is best not to publish all the reasons why a coin is fake because next time the forger will try to correct all his mistakes and it will be more difficult to recognise his work as fake! So, it is best only to publish some - but not all -details about how to identify the fakes.
