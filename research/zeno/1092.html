You are right; this listing is a handy thing to have. I have added it to my "Favorites" list. I doubt he put the list together from scratch. There are numerous books of rulers ("king lists") -- I have several in my library, including one just for Islamic kingdoms and one just for China, Japan, Korea and Vietnam. The latter was compiled by a Chinese Jesuit, published about 1910, and is in French! It is still the best work on Chinese dating that I have seen, and I have 3,000 books on Chinese history in my library. Not only does it include all the dynasties and petty kingdoms, but also includes the rulers of numerous states during the Chou dynasty. The French spelling of the names would be difficult to follow, but the Chinese characters make the name clear.

Dear bcebeci: The books you asked about are:
The Islamic Dynasties - A Chronological & Genealogical Handbook by Clifford E. Bosworth, Edinburgh Univ. Press 1967, 245 pages (also labeled Islamic Surveys Volume 5). Two other good books are: Monarchs, Rulers, Dynasties & Kingdoms of the World by R. F. Tapsell London 1983, 511 pages; and Handbook of Oriental History by C. H. Philips, Royal Historical Society, London 1951 (reprint 1963) 265 pages. I have all of these books.
