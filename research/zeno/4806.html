hfrans:
The dating of Iraq b Mansur seems not quite clear. In an article by M Fedorov; "The Khwarazmshahs of the Banu Iraq", in Journal of Persian Studies, Fedorov writes: "...the Samanid Ismail had sent a force to Khwarazm and installed there his own nominee (as governor), Iraq or Araq b Mansur, so that in this year 293 / 896 the future founder of the Iraq line enters the historical scene."
Of course, Iraq b Mansur might have been ruling in Khwarezm earlier, but been replaced by the Saffarid Amr b al-Layth, and in AH 293 reinstalled by the samanids?
Anyway, this coin might be dated AH 280. Is there anyone who would try to read the obverse field legend?
