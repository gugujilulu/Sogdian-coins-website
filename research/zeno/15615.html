Sale 5003 Lot 335.
Arab-Khwarezm, al-Fadl bin Sulaymán at-Túsí as governor of Khorásán (782/3-787/ 166-171 AH), AR Drachm, undated, bust of king right wearing flat crown with wave effect on crest, nape guard and cheek strapsrev. rider carrying weapon in left hand, mounted on horse walking right, al-Fadl in Kufic script above horse's back (Mitch.500b; ONS Newsletter #179, Spring 2004, "Money Circulation of Khwarezm in the early-medieval period", Michael Fedorov, p.16-23, Type Γ VIδ), hoard staining on edges of obverse flan, spots of corrosion on reverse, but otherwise fine/good fine, extremely rare
Estimate £ 2,000-2,200
