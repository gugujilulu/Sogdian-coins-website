Thank you!
I have received notice from Aleksander Naymark:
Most recent (albeit brief) summary of attempts to read legends on Bukharan coins imitating Euthydemus types see in my article on early Sogdian imitation coinages in issue 1 of 2008 Vestnik Drevnei Istorii. This particular coin can be dated to the the middle or second half of the 1st century C.E. About the legends: on the right there is distorted Greek; on the left (from down up) MR'Y. Ductus is very crude, last letter did not come out well and might be dubious, but first three letters can be identified with iron certainty.
А.И. Наймарк. О причинах появления подражаний эллинистическим монетам в Согдиане // Вестник Древней Истории, 2008, № 1
(Naymark A. About the reasons of appearance of Hellenistic coins imitations in Soghdiana) // Vestnik Drevnei Istorii, 2008, #1).
