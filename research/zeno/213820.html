Ordos Culture large bronze feline harness fitting
144 mm, 113.61 gr

Ordos Culture bronze harness fitting, cast in open work depicting a feline figure with a serpent projecting from mouth, another serpent biting the animals tail. Rare piece in exceptional museum grade.

Dating to circa the second half of the 1st millennium B.C. Steppes region, Mongolia.

Ex- Walter G. von Krenner collection, acquired in the 1970's.
