Of course, you know Turgesh's tamgha on all coins assigned to Turgeshes? This is runic character T considered as first letter in the name Turgesh. Basing on a similar idea Dr.V.Nastich suggested that runic letter R (similar to Latin letter M) means the first consonant letter in the name Arslanid, taking into account that ruler's name Arslan can be read on coins (in Sogdian script) and that Arslan should be representative of the small Turgesh's branch Arslanids.
