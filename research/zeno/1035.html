This is not a Sogdian coin but an early Islamic coin. The word above the horse does indeed appear clearly as Arabic "nasaf". More importantly is the inscription on the reverse, albeit somewhat primitive, but very clearly Arabic "Muhammad rasul Allah". I do not know when Nasaf was first occupied by the Arabs, and Minorsky's article in the "Enyclopedia of Islam" does not mention its early Islamic history. I would suspect either a very late Umayyad period, circa AH115 or later, or early Abbasid, likely prior to the more traditional Abbasid type dated AH157.
