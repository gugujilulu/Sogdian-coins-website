SOGHD: Anonymous, 7th/8th century, AE cash (3.96g), Zeno-100603 (this piece), Fisher's Ding-741, standard Tang Dynasty kai yuan tong bao obverse, Sogdian tamgha at the right on the reverse, choice VF, RRR, ex Shawn Hamilton Collection. The reverse tamgha is unique to this type and has not been assigned to a specific location, generally classified in the Soghd region, which includes Bukhara, Samarqand, and several other identified locations.
