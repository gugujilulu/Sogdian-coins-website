I consider this type to be the branch between the last of the Afrighid drachms citing Azkaswar on the obverse, either al-Abrad or 'Akki on the obvese, and the first of the Ghuzz of Syr Darya with only the name of 'Abd Allah b. Tahir (brother and successor of Tahla in AH 213 / 828) on the obverse. The weight of 1.95g fits into the late Afrighid drachms and at the heaviest examples of the Ghuzz.
Talha was killed in battle in Sistan in 213 / 828 and succeeded by his brother 'Abd Allah b. Tahir, who is cited on the obverse of all other issues of the Ghuzz. the coins of 'Abd Allah b. Tahir NEVER cite Azkaswar.
