Photo Details
Thick and heavy specimen. Compare with Z#33694, 189825, 189826 and 189860.

Metal composition is : Cu 89.1810, Zn 0.3187, Sn 3.7445, As 0.7834, Sb 0.9928, Pb 2.7717, Fe 0.1788, Mn 1.7085, Ni 0.3105, Cd,Co,Bi all are under detection limits, Sum 99.9899 %, XRF data. So we see As-Sb bearing, low led and low tin bronze. Manganese content may be connected with attempts of previous owner to darken the coin with using of KMnO4+CuSO4 solution. Patination is sulphide type - in some points sulphur content reach up to 3 %.
