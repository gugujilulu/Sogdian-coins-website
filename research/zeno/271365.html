@Tamgha resebles tamgha of some atabages.
IMHO, coin looks doubtful, but let's wait more opinions.

Originally Genghis Khan's tamgha was used by his descendants in Toluid line, including Hülegü, Möngke and Qublay, but not by any "atabages". Cf. who atabegs were (Not Mongols).
Let's hope it is not a fantasy, but a new type, perhaps from Eastern Turkestan/Tibet.

@The script doesn't looks as Mongol.

Probably not Mongol script derived from an old Uyghur script, but a script created by Tibetan monk Drogön Chögyal Phagpa for Qublay, known as ʼPhags-pa script.
