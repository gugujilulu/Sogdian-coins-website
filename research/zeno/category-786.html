Countermarks on Kobadien drachms
Kob1 Kob2 Kob3 Kob4 Kob5 Kob6 Kob7
Göbl 73 Göbl 72 - - - - Göbl 42var

      As far as it is known, three hoards of Kobadien billon Peroz imitations were found:
- Hoard 1 (Termez hoard?), published by R Göbl in his "Dokumente zur Geschichte der Iranischen Hunnen ...", 1967. 53 coins with images could be seen on Tafel 88-91: billon Peroz imitations with two tamghas and a Bactrian word in the obverse margin, all engraved on the die, two portrait cm‖s, Kob1 (Göbl 73) on the obverse, Kob2 (Göbl 72) on the reverse.
- Hoard 2, found 2001/2. Similar coins as above (including countermarks), but now with an additional 3rd cm Kob3: "forepart of a camel + elephant head".
- Hoard 3, about 500 coins, found in SE South Central Asia in 2013. Same coins as Hoard 2, but with additional cm’s: 4th cm Kob4: facing beast on obverse, 5th cm Kob5: animal head (like Göbl 107 but facing left) on reverse; see also Zeno #144679.
      Besides these, there were a 6th cm Kob6: the portrait of a Bactrian soldier, which appeared on five of the coins, and on one coin was a 7th cm Kob7: the Arab word "ja’iz".
      Some of the coins from Hoards 2 and 3 are uploaded to Zeno, in Kobadien category. See also images of 126 specimens from the Hoard 3.

     Håkan Fransson
