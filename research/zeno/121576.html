Laosa » 6-February-2013 7:51pm
It's also a universal formula for any multinational country with different ethnic groups. In Russia we also have national minorities (i.e. 'non-Russians'), I think that in France, for instance, there are numerous national minorities as well, etc. And any 'national minority' translates into Chinese as 少数民族, and vice versa. And it is not correct from both linguistical and political points of view to translate the above mentioned description (少数民族地区方孔铜钱) as “China national minority region coin”.
