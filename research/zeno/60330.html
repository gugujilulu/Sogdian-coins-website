Swastika is really not common at pre-Mongol Islamic coppers of the area. It can be seen at:
- Qarakhanid fals from Ferghana or Issyk-Qul area;
- Khwarizm fulus (##52088, 21172, 23003 and 46551);
- pre-Islamic Khwarizm coppers, directly related to the above Khwarizm fulus type with horse and solar character (swastika).
Swastika at your coin is more similar to the latest.
