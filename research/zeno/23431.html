Correction: This coin was purchased from a now notorious dealer of Central Asian forgeries, Igor Khabak, who sells regularly on ebay as kh.andson.

Address: 31284 Huntley Sq # 1122
Beverly Hills, MI 48025, USA.

He in turn purchased this and dozens, perhaps hundreds, of central Asian forgeries from Oleg Lyebchenko. Igor Khabak also sells forged cast bronzes from Sogdiana, and a number of Bukharkhoda AR drachms cast from the same mould. This coin was sold by Khabak to another reputable numismatist and scholar in the field, returned as a fake, and resold. Igor Khabak returned less than half of the money paid, which seems to be his standard practice. More than half a dozen complaints have been filed against him. He continuous to sell such coins on ebay. Reputable Sogdian numismatists assert that even the low grade Sogdiana (Chach, Sogdiana, etc.) bronzes which he sells are forgeries.
