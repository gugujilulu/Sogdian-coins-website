I know well the origin and the whole history of this particular coin.
The tamga on this coin has nothing in common with the tyurgesh tamga.
Yes, there are all sorts of nuances with the arrangement of letters and so on on Chach coins.
The coin just turned out that way. This happens sometimes.
If it were a tyurgesh tamga, it would definitely be indicated in the lot description.
The seller has been in this topic for 23 years and has good consultants.
It’s just that no one began to deceive you by saying that this is tamga tyurgesh, but they wrote the truth.
If the seller had mentioned tyurgesh tamga, the sale would have been 10-20 times more.
This is a regular Chach coin.
