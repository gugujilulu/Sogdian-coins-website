Av. On the center of an inscription here is the word х’γ’n «Qaghan» with Soghdian italics. Rv. On the center of an anchor-shaped tamgha, Soghdian legend trδw «Tardu» is placed in one level (layer). Perhaps these coins were issued by the founder of the Western Turkic Qaghanate Tardu Qaghan (Chin.: Da-tu ko-han; Byzantine: Taρδου - 576-603).
