If “fake” there should be an original which was imitated. In any case this coin-type is not s.c. “fantasy”, i.e. never existed authentic coin.

Why? Simply, because originally this type was published by Sergej Pavlovič Tolstov from controlled archaeological excavations and republished by Vainberg and others, mostly in different auctions…

Frankly, I don’t think that specimens in the Zeno’s database are s.c. “fakes”. Without putting all specimens in one subcategory, there can’t be differentiated authentic items from imitations.
